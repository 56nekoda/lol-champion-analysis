THE MASTER OF SHADOWS / ASSASSIN

ZED

Leader of the Order of Shadow · Ionia

01 / ABILITIES

SHADOW TECHNIQUES.

PASSIVE — CONTEMPT FOR THE WEAK

Zed's basic attacks deal additional magic damage to enemies who are low on health. This effect cannot repeatedly trigger on the same champion within a short period.

Q — RAZOR SHURIKEN

Zed and his shadows throw spinning shurikens in a direction, damaging enemies they pass through.

W — LIVING SHADOW

Zed creates a shadow that can mimic his basic abilities. He can reactivate the ability to switch positions with the shadow.

E — SHADOW SLASH

Zed and his shadows slash nearby enemies, dealing damage. Enemies hit by a shadow's slash are slowed.

R — DEATH MARK

Zed dashes through an enemy champion and marks them. After a short delay, the mark detonates and deals additional damage based on the damage Zed dealt while the mark was active.

02 / CHAMPION ANALYSIS

MASTER THE SHADOWS.

STRENGTHS

High burst damage, strong mobility, and the ability to threaten vulnerable enemy carries. His shadows create multiple angles for attacks and escapes.

WEAKNESSES

Zed relies on landing skillshots and managing his energy. Crowd control and defensive items can make it difficult for him to eliminate targets.

PLAYSTYLE

Look for opportunities to pressure isolated targets. Use Living Shadow to create attack angles, then combine your abilities to deal burst damage.

TEAM FIGHTS

Wait for important enemy abilities to be used before diving into the backline. Choose your target carefully and keep an escape route in mind.
