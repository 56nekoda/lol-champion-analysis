THE UNFORGOTTEN / FIGHTER · ASSASSIN

YONE

Two blades · One spirit · An endless hunt

01 / ABILITIES

DUAL BLADES.

PASSIVE — WAY OF THE HUNTER

Yone alternates between two swords. Every second attack deals additional magic damage, and he gains increased critical strike chance.

Q — MORTAL STEEL

Yone thrusts forward with his sword, damaging enemies in a line. Repeated hits build stacks that empower the next cast with a forward dash and knock-up.

W — SPIRIT CLEAVE

Yone swings both swords in a cone, damaging enemies and granting himself a shield when the attack connects.

E — SOUL UNBOUND

Yone leaves his body behind and enters Spirit Form, gaining movement speed. When the effect ends, he returns to his body and deals additional damage based on the damage stored during the ability.

R — FATE SEALED

Yone dashes forward, striking enemies in his path and pulling them toward him. Enemies hit are also knocked airborne.

02 / CHAMPION ANALYSIS

WALK BETWEEN WORLDS.

STRENGTHS

Strong dueling potential, high mobility, mixed damage, and powerful team fight engage. His ultimate can create opportunities to catch multiple enemies.

WEAKNESSES

Yone can be vulnerable when his mobility abilities are unavailable. His aggressive engages can also leave him exposed if the timing is poor.

PLAYSTYLE

Build up Mortal Steel stacks and look for opportunities to engage. Use Soul Unbound to create pressure while keeping your return position in mind.

TEAM FIGHTS

Look for grouped enemies and use Fate Sealed to initiate or follow up on crowd control. Coordinate your engage with your teammates.
