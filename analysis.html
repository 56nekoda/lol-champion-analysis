UNDERSTANDING CHAMPION TYPES

HOW DO THEY FIGHT?

Every champion has a different combat style. These categories help explain how they approach battles and contribute to their team.

MAGE

Mages use magical abilities to deal damage, often from a distance. Many can also slow, stun, or control enemies to create opportunities for their team.

EXAMPLE: NEEKO

ASSASSIN

Assassins are fast and mobile champions who specialize in quickly eliminating important enemy targets. They often rely on surprise attacks and high burst damage.

EXAMPLE: ZED

SKIRMISHER

Skirmishers are close-range fighters who excel in extended battles. They use mobility, repeated attacks, and defensive abilities to defeat enemies one-on-one.

EXAMPLE: YONE

FIGHTER

Fighters combine strong attacks with the ability to survive damage. They specialize in close combat and can remain dangerous during longer fights.

EXAMPLE: IRELIA
