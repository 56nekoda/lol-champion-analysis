THE BLADE DANCER / FIGHTER · ASSASSIN

IRELIA

The graceful blades of Ionia

01 / ABILITIES

DANCE OF BLADES.

PASSIVE — IONIAN FERVOR

Hitting enemies with abilities grants Irelia stacking attack speed. At maximum stacks, her attacks deal additional damage.

Q — BLADESURGE

Irelia dashes to a target, dealing damage and healing herself. The ability can be used again when it kills the target or hits a marked enemy.

W — DEFIANT DANCE

Irelia charges and releases a blade strike. While charging, she reduces incoming physical damage and cannot be interrupted by most crowd control.

E — FLAWLESS DUET

Irelia sends two blades to chosen locations. When they meet, they damage and stun enemies caught between them, marking them for Bladesurge.

R — VANGUARD'S EDGE

Irelia launches a storm of blades that damages and marks enemy champions. The blades form a wall that damages and slows enemies who pass through it.

02 / CHAMPION ANALYSIS

CONTROL THE FLOW.

STRENGTHS

Excellent mobility around minion waves, strong dueling potential, and powerful sustained damage when her passive is fully stacked.

WEAKNESSES

Irelia depends on landing abilities and managing her passive stacks. Missing her stun or using Bladesurge without a reset can limit her options.

PLAYSTYLE

Use minions and marked targets to move around fights. Build passive stacks before committing and look for opportunities to reset Bladesurge.

TEAM FIGHTS

Use Vanguard's Edge to create openings for your team. Follow up with Bladesurge on marked enemies and keep moving through the fight.
