THE CURIOUS CHAMELEON / MAGE · SUPPORT

NEEKO

Curiosity, deception, and magic

01 / ABILITIES

MAGIC OF DECEPTION.

PASSIVE — INHERENT GLAMOUR

Neeko can disguise herself as an allied champion or certain units on the map. Her disguise breaks when she takes specific actions or receives certain effects.

Q — BLOOMING BURST

Neeko throws a seed to a target location, dealing magic damage in an area. The seed can bloom again when it hits champions or large monsters, or kills an enemy.

W — SHAPESPLITTER

Neeko creates a clone of herself while briefly becoming invisible and gaining movement speed. Her clone can move separately to confuse enemies.

E — TANGLE-BARBS

Neeko launches a magical spiral that damages and roots enemies. Hitting an enemy increases the projectile's size and the duration of its root.

R — POP BLOSSOM

Neeko gathers spiritual energy before leaping into the air and landing with a burst of magic. Nearby enemies are knocked up and then stunned.

02 / CHAMPION ANALYSIS

TRICK YOUR ENEMIES.

STRENGTHS

Strong crowd control, surprising engage potential, and the ability to confuse enemies with disguises and clones. Her ultimate can change the outcome of team fights.

WEAKNESSES

Neeko has limited mobility and can be vulnerable when her crowd control abilities are unavailable. Her ultimate also requires careful timing and positioning.

PLAYSTYLE

Use disguises and Shapesplitter to create unexpected angles. Land Tangle-Barbs to control enemies and follow up with your damage.

TEAM FIGHTS

Look for opportunities to catch multiple enemies with Pop Blossom. Use fog of war, disguises, and your clone to make your engage harder to predict.
